Privacy policy

Jadual is a class timetable app for Android. It is built so that your timetable never leaves your phone. This page explains exactly what the app stores, what it sends, and why.

Who is responsible

Jadual is developed by Haziq, an individual developer. Contact: haziq.support.dev@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your timetable, subjects and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • There is no account and no sign-up.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers.
  • The only data that leaves your phone is what Google Play needs to process a purchase of Jadual Pro, described below.

What is stored on your phone

Your semesters, subjects, class times, rooms, lecturer names, archived semesters and settings (theme, language, time format and similar). This data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. We cannot see it.

Files the app writes

When you choose Save image in the wallpaper maker, Jadual saves a picture of your timetable to Pictures/Jadual. When you export a semester, it saves a .jadual file where you choose and a copy in Download/Jadual. These files stay on your phone unless you share them yourself.

Purchases

Jadual is free to use. Jadual Pro is sold through Google Play, which acts as the merchant and processes your payment under Google's privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

To check whether Pro is unlocked, the app uses RevenueCat as a processor. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt from Google Play and a random, anonymous app user ID generated on your phone. It does not receive your name, email or timetable. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionWhy
Set wallpaperTo put your timetable on the lock screen or home screen when you ask.
Run at startupTo keep reminders and the morning wallpaper redraw scheduled after the phone restarts.
NotificationsTo show class and exam reminders. Android asks you first, and you can turn it off at any time.
Alarms and remindersTo ring a reminder at the minute you chose. Without it, reminders still arrive, a few minutes early.
Internet and network stateOnly for checking and restoring a Pro purchase with Google Play and RevenueCat.
Google Play billingTo buy Jadual Pro.
Keep awake and background workUsed by Android's scheduler to redraw the wallpaper at 06:00.

None of these permissions give the app access to your contacts, location, photos, messages or other apps.

Export and deletion

You can export any semester to a plain file from Settings at any time, and import it on another phone. The file goes wherever you choose to save or share it; the app does not upload it anywhere.

Uninstalling Jadual, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes everything the app stored except files you saved to Pictures or Download. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing further for us to delete. For purchase records held by Google Play or RevenueCat, email us and we will help with a deletion request.

Children

Jadual is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
